OurIntelList

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This policy explains how OurIntelList handles information when you use the Android application. OurIntelList helps people create and share shopping lists. It applies to information processed through the app and its synchronization services.

Information we handle

When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication provides the app with your account identifier, email address, and, when available, display name. The app uses these details to identify your account, match invitations, and show member information to people who share lists with you.

Content you create may include list and item names, quantities, units, notes, categories, item status, barcode values, purchase history, and sharing invitations. The app also stores the email addresses you invite so you can find those contacts when sharing another list. Members of a shared list can see its content and the membership information needed to use that list.

The app keeps a local copy of list and account data in its private Android storage so lists work offline. When connected, shared list and account data is synchronized using Google Firebase Firestore. Shopping trips are stored locally and are not synchronized. The app does not separately encrypt the local database.

Camera and barcode scanning

Camera access is optional and is used only when you scan a barcode. Barcode frames are analyzed on your device using Google ML Kit. The app does not save or upload camera images or video. If you associate a scanned barcode with an item, its barcode value is stored with that list item and synchronized with people who can access the list.

How information is used and shared

We use information to provide sign-in, offline list management, sharing, synchronization, barcode lookup, and purchase-based suggestions. Google Firebase Authentication and Firestore process account and synchronized list data to provide these features. We do not sell personal information, serve ads, use advertising tracking, or include analytics or push notifications.

When you share a list, its content and relevant membership details are available to people with access to that list. Invite only people you intend to share it with. Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policy while providing Firebase services.

Account deletion requests

You can request deletion of your OurIntelList account even if you no longer have the app installed. Email the privacy contact below from the Google account email address you use to sign in to OurIntelList, and state that you want your account deleted. Do not include your password. We may contact you to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

After verification, we will delete the Firebase Authentication account, lists you own and their contents, your profile and personal cloud data, and your memberships and pending invitations. Lists owned by another person are not deleted: your access and membership are removed, while the list and its shared content remain available to its owner and other members. An email request does not erase local copies already stored on devices. To remove a local copy, use the in-app account-deletion option before submitting the request, or clear OurIntelList's app data on each device. Uninstalling alone does not delete cloud data.

To submit a request: email codeechodevteam@gmail.com with the Google account email address associated with your OurIntelList account.

Deletion from the app and retention

You can delete your account from Delete account in the signed-in account menu. The app asks you to reauthenticate with Google, then removes your Firebase Authentication account, lists you own and their contents, your profile and personal cloud data, your memberships and invitations, and account-scoped local data. Lists owned by someone else remain for their owner and members. Cached content from lists owned by others may remain on this device and is available only to another account with access to those lists. If deletion cannot be completed, your account remains active and the app lets you retry. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete cloud data.

Security

Information sent to Firebase is transmitted using Google's transport security and protected by Firebase access controls. The local database is stored in the app's private storage but is not separately encrypted by the app. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your Google account and device.

Children

OurIntelList is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.
